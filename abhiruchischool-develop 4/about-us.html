About Us

Abhiruchi Gurukul School

Founded in 2013 under Shree Dnyanyog Vidya Pratishthan, our school was established by Shri Sunil Bhide in memory of his father, with a vision to blend modern education with cultural values. For over 12 years, Abhiruchi Gurukul has made quality education accessible to families across Pune.

We are a trusted choice for parents seeking academic excellence and holistic development. Located in Pune, we are a CBSE-proposed co-educational school offering classes from Nursery to Grade X. At Abhiruchi Gurukul, learning goes beyond textbooks—children learn by doing, exploring and discovering the world around them.

Our Vision

To build a learning ecosystem where modern education meets timeless tradition—a place where every child gets the chance to discover their true calling and is inspired to grow as a creative thinker, innovator and responsible green leader, ready to face global challenges ahead with confidence, compassion and resilience.

Our Mission

Our mission is to nurture knowledgeable, responsible and compassionate learners who understand their role in creating a sustainable future. Through experiential learning, we connect lessons from nature with real-world challenges, preparing students to succeed in life while staying rooted in values.

Education Philosophy

Sustainable Living Approach

At Abhiruchi Gurukul, sustainability is more than a concept—it’s a way of life. We provide children with a natural, eco-friendly environment where they learn the value of mindful living.

Cultural Rootedness

We believe education is incomplete without a strong connection to one’s roots. By celebrating festivals in their traditional form, children experience the joy, values and lessons embedded in our culture.

Sanskar Varg

Our Shloka recitation sessions form an integral part of learning. Regular chanting not only improves concentration, memory and pronunciation, but also instills values of patience, empathy and inner calm.

Experiential Learning

We educate kids who learn by building, creating, experimenting, or exploring. Experiential learning for children is all about learning through direct experience rather than just listening or watching.

Public Speaking

At our school, we believe that every student deserves the opportunity to express themselves and build confidence through public speaking. As a part of our Public Speaking Program.

Holistic Learning Approach

Head

Develops critical thinking and knowledge through learning and understanding concepts.

Heart

Fosters emotional intelligence, empathy and values to build character and compassion for lifelong learning and growth.

Hand

Applies knowledge through skills, creativity and practical experiences for real-world learning.
