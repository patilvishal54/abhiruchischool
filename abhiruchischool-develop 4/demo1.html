Welcome to Abhiruchi Gurukul School

Founded in 2013 under Shree Dnyanyog Vidya Pratishthan, our school was established by Shri Sunil Bhide in memory of his father, with a vision to blend modern education with cultural values. For over 12 years, Abhiruchi Gurukul has made quality education accessible to families across Pune.

We are a trusted choice for parents seeking academic excellence and holistic development. Located in Pune, we are a CBSE-proposed co-educational school offering classes from Nursery to Grade X. At Abhiruchi Gurukul, learning goes beyond textbooks—children learn by doing, exploring and discovering the world around them.

A Day in the Life of Our Students

Morning Assembly

The day starts with prayers, yoga and meditation, setting a calm yet energized tone. Yoga builds focus and stamina, while meditation improves emotional balance and concentration helping children start their day with positivity.

Exercise & Games

Physical fitness is a daily priority. Students engage in warm-ups, stretches, athletics and fun games to build strength. Traditional Indian games like Kho-Kho and Kabaddi instill teamwork, discipline and cultural connection.

Learning Knowledge

Instead of rote learning, lessons are brought to life with storytelling methods. Stories spark imagination, deepen understanding and make every subject more engaging, memorable, fun and truly meaningful for students.

Activities & Co-Curriculars

Children explore Drama, Karate, Skating, Taekwondo and other activities that help them build confidence, creativity and leadership skills while nurturing holistic growth and personal development for every child.

Experiential Learning

At Abhiruchi, we believe children learn best through hands-on experiences. Whether it's building, experimenting, or exploring, students gain practical knowledge and problem-solving by actively engaging with their environment.

Balanced Meals

Nutritious meals are served daily, with vegetables grown in our own school garden. This not only ensures fresh and healthy food but also teaches children the value of labour, patience and gratitude as they experience the journey.

Gardening & Nature Walks

Children spend time in our vegetable gardens, composting units and outdoor spaces where they learn to care for plants, observe insects and understand ecosystems. Regular nature walks deepen their love for the environment.

Sanskar Varg

The day winds down with Sanskar Varg, where children recite shlokas, learn moral values and connect with cultural roots. Shloka chanting improves memory, focus and speech clarity while nurturing respect for tradition and inner discipline.

Our Learning Focus

At Abhiruchi School, education goes beyond academics. We focus on nurturing the mind, body and character of every child through a balanced approach rooted in Indian culture, scientific curiosity and real-world experiences.

Physical Fitness – Healthy Body, Healthy Mind

Each day begins with yoga, meditation and Omkar recitation practices deeply connected to Indian tradition. These sessions help children develop focus, resilience and inner calm, promoting long-term health and emotional balance.

Cultural Rootedness – Values with Science

Festivals are celebrated as living lessons. From Dev Diwali and Kande Navmi to Dussehra and Diwali, students learn the cultural meaning and scientific reasoning behind traditions.

Empathy Through Co-habitation – Living with Animals

Children share their school environment with cats, cows, geese and dogs. Caring for them builds responsibility, compassion and respect for all living beings.

Public Speaking – Voices with Confidence

Students participate in discussions and term-end stage presentations, building confidence, clarity and courage to express their ideas openly.

Naturalistic Learners – Growing with Nature

Students work in vegetable gardens, observing growth cycles and harvesting produce, nurturing patience, curiosity and responsibility through hands-on learning.

Scientific Approach – Curiosity Turned Into Discovery

From preschool onward, children perform hands-on experiments that spark curiosity, encourage questioning and build strong problem-solving skills.
