Campus Features

Play Areas

Physical growth is just as important as academics. Our campus has safe and joyful play areas with slides, swings, see-saws and merry-go-rounds, where children spend time every day. Play helps them build strength, coordination and a happy, active lifestyle.

Forest Area

The serene Forest Area within our campus provides a natural learning space for environmental activities and outdoor study sessions. It inspires students to appreciate nature, understand ecosystems and develop a sense of environmental responsibility.

Amphitheatre

Creativity finds its stage here. Our open-air amphitheatre hosts annual events, cultural programs, competitions and performances like drama, dance and shloka recitation—helping children gain confidence and express themselves freely.

Vegetable Garden with Meandering Water Play Area

The vegetable garden is a living classroom. Students grow their own fruits and vegetables, care for plants and even observe insects to understand their role in the ecosystem. Gardening also strengthens motor skills, improves focus and builds patience.

Sports Facilities

Basketball Court

The school's Basketball Court encourages teamwork, discipline and sportsmanship. Students actively participate in practice sessions and inter-house matches.

Tennis Court

Our Tennis Court offers students the opportunity to learn and enjoy the sport in a well-maintained facility. It helps develop concentration and coordination.

Futsal Court

Our modern Futsal Court provides students with an exciting space to enjoy fast-paced football action, improving coordination and quick decision-making skills.

Kho-Kho & Kabaddi Ground

Our well-maintained ground encourages students to engage in traditional Indian sports, building team spirit, agility and strategic thinking.

Running Track

Our 100m and 200m Running Track is ideal for athletics training and inter-house sports events, motivating students to build stamina and passion for fitness.

Sand Pit

The sand pit encourages sensory play and social interaction, helping children develop motor skills, creativity, teamwork and problem-solving abilities.

Academic Facilities

Smart Learning Labs

Our Smart Learning Labs include advanced Computer and Robotics sections for hands-on exploration. Students learn coding, automation and technology

Library

Our spacious and well-stocked library serves as a hub of knowledge with a wide range of books, magazines and digital resources to support learning.

Well-Lit Classroom

Bright, spacious classrooms designed to keep learning interactive and engaging. Each classroom is built to spark curiosity and participation.

Multipurpose Hall

Our spacious hall is designed for school assemblies, cultural events, competitions and seminars with comfortable and well-equipped facilities.

Skill Development Room

A creative space for hands-on learning – from art and design to robotics and workshops – where students discover passions and build real-world skills.

Subject Labs

Well-equipped labs for Geography, Science (Biology, Physics, Chemistry), Mathematics and Vocational studies. Each lab promotes practical learning.

Additional Facilities

Exhibition Spaces

Dedicated spaces where students display projects, artwork and creative ideas, inspiring innovation, confidence and collaboration.

Dining Area

A cheerful and hygienic place where students enjoy healthy meals together, building friendships over shared tables.

Sit-Out Area

A cozy spot for students and teachers to relax, chat, or just enjoy a quiet break in the open air.

VIP Lounge

A warm and welcoming space for parents, guests and dignitaries visiting the campus.

Medical Room

Our well-equipped Medical Room ensures prompt first aid and healthcare assistance for students during school hours.

Security & Parking

CCTV surveillance and RFID-based entry system ensure constant monitoring and controlled access for a safe environment.
