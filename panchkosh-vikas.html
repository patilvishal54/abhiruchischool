Panchkosh Vikas

Panchkosh Vikas – A Holistic Development Approach

Panchakosha theory in education, derived from ancient Indian philosophy, emphasizes holistic development by considering five layers or sheaths of the individual: physical, vital, emotional, intellectual and spiritual. It suggests that true education involves nurturing all aspects of a child’s being, not just cognitive skills.

Annamaya Kosha

This is the outermost layer made up of the physical body, sustained by food. It includes bones, muscles, skin and all bodily organs. At our school, providing nutritious meals, balanced with carbohydrates, proteins, fats, minerals and vitamins, helps children maintain good health through proper nutrition, exercise and rest.

Pranamaya Kosha

This kosha consists of life force (prana) flowing through the body via breath and energy channels. Breathing exercises, yoga, meditation and Indian outdoor games at our school balance this energy, improving mental well-being, focus and emotional strength.

Manomaya Kosha

This kosha governs the mind, emotions and thoughts. At our school, we nurture mindfulness through storytelling, meditation, festival celebrations and nature interactions such as animal petting. These experiences help children develop emotional balance, clarity and strong social bonds.

Vigyanmaya Kosha

The sheath of wisdom, intelligence and inner knowledge. At school, critical thinking, problem-solving, robotics, science experiments and moral education strengthen this kosha. Activities like Maths Lab, AV learning and robotics prepare children to become thoughtful, responsible citizens.

Anandamaya Kosha

The innermost kosha where the soul experiences joy and bliss. At our school, meditation, art, music, dance, picnics, fun days and playful activities help children connect to inner happiness, building confidence and contentment.

"Education is complete only when it nurtures body, mind, intellect, emotions and spirit together."
