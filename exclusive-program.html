Exclusive Program

A Holistic Start to Every Day

Our mornings begin with yoga and meditation, setting the tone for a calm mind and active body. Throughout the day, children engage in interactive classroom sessions, indoor and outdoor play and experiential learning activities that spark curiosity.

Building Skills for Life

From self-study habits to teamwork, every activity is designed to develop both academic skills and life skills. Fine motor skills are nurtured through engaging tasks like writing in sand, working with clay and tracing sandpaper letters, making learning hands-on and joyful.

Learning with Nature

At Abhiruchi Gurukul, children learn to grow with nature. Activities like gardening and animal petting help them develop empathy, responsibility and a deep bond with the environment.

Program Features

Advanced AV Learning

Our school uses advanced audio-visual learning tools to make lessons more interactive and engaging. Smart classrooms and multimedia content enhance creativity.

Agricultural Experience

Students get hands-on agricultural experience through planting, composting and crop care, learning sustainability and respect for the environment.

Other School Activities

Even after school hours, learning continues with self-study habits and various extracurricular activities that let children discover their passions.

Preparing for the Future

We encourage participation in external examinations like Silver Olympiad, Science Olympiad Foundation exams and scholarship tests to strengthen knowledge.

All-round Development

We understand each child grows differently. Our program focuses on nurturing individual talents while ensuring overall academic, physical, emotional and development.

Beyond the Classroom

Students can choose from a wide range of extracurricular activities after school, encouraging creativity, confidence and exploration of new interests.
